Encrypted DMs

For private conversations we recommend Signal. When both people use Fastcord, a DM can also be end-to-end encrypted with Signal's published protocols, so Discord relays it but can't read it.

PQXDHSignal's post-quantum key agreement, with X25519 and ML-KEM-768.
Double RatchetA new key for every message, with ChaCha20-Poly1305.
Safety numbersCompare 60 digits to confirm who you're talking to.

From the next release, for one-on-one DMs: text, files, replies, edits, deletes and reactions. Discord still sees who you message and when, and roughly how large each message and file is. Prekeys signed with SLH-DSA and the Triple Ratchet come next.

In progress: self-hosted servers, with federated routing between them so no central service is needed.
